# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Helpful behavior includes being constructive, assuming
good intent, respecting different experience levels, protecting other people's privacy, and accepting feedback
gracefully.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing another person's
private information, deliberately sharing sensitive activity data, or disrupting project collaboration.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces representing the
project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates these standards.

Report serious conduct concerns privately through
<https://github.com/vietvh-3042/activity-journal/security/advisories/new>. Do not include unnecessary personal or
activity data. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and handled proportionally.
